Welcome to the Cadet TV Personality Awards

Where Young Voices Rise and Future Media Leaders Emerge.

Every great broadcaster begins with a voice. Every confident leader begins with an opportunity.

The Cadet TV Personality Awards, an initiative of Cadet Media Ghana, is a youth-focused educational and media excellence programme dedicated to discovering, nurturing, and celebrating outstanding broadcasting talents among schoolchildren across Ghana.

Through practical television news presentations, journalism, reporting, media production, and creative storytelling, the awards give young people an opportunity to demonstrate their abilities, develop confidence, strengthen communication skills, and embrace responsible leadership.

Beyond celebrating talent, the programme promotes education, discipline, teamwork, creativity, and national responsibility, preparing young people to become confident communicators and responsible citizens.

We are not simply celebrating today's young talents. We are inspiring tomorrow's broadcasters, communicators, and leaders.

What Makes the Awards Special

01

Discovering the Next Generation of Broadcasters

A national platform where young talents demonstrate their abilities in television news anchoring, reporting, production, and creative communication.

02

Empowering Students Through Media Education

Building confidence, leadership, critical thinking, teamwork, and public speaking skills through practical media experiences.

03

Promoting Safety Awareness and Responsible Citizenship

Integrating safety education through the National Academic Assessment Drive (NAAD) Safety Edition to equip young people with essential knowledge about personal safety, emergency preparedness, and responsible behaviour.

04

Recognising Excellence and Celebrating Achievement

Honouring outstanding students and participating schools for their creativity, dedication, teamwork, and excellence in broadcasting.

05

Connecting Schools, Families, and Institutions

Bringing together students, educators, parents, media professionals, and national institutions to support youth development and educational excellence.

Education Through Media: NAAD Safety Edition

The Cadet TV Personality Awards is implemented under the National Academic Assessment Drive (NAAD), using media to nurture young talent and promote safety education. Every nominee automatically receives the NAAD drive, which carries safety lessons from national safety and security agencies and an assessment quiz. Cadets who succeed in the quiz are awarded.
