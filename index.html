About the Awards

The Cadet TV Personality Awards is an initiative by Cadet Media Ghana, aimed at discovering, nurturing, and celebrating young broadcasting talent in Ghana. The program provides a platform for students to develop and showcase their skills in journalism, news reporting, and media production.

Read more about us →

Education Through Media

The Cadet TV Personality Awards is implemented under the National Academic Assessment Drive (NAAD), using media to nurture young talent and promote safety education. Every nominee automatically receives the NAAD drive, which carries safety lessons from national safety and security agencies and an assessment quiz. Cadets who succeed in the quiz are awarded.
